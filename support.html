Support

Email pharaohmo21@gmail.com. Please include your device (for example iPhone 16 or Pixel 9), the game version (shown in Settings) and what happened. Never send passwords or payment card details.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts or cloud saves, so deleting the game deletes your progress. Moving to a new device does not carry progress over.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game while online: it checks for purchases that were paid for but not yet delivered, and delivers them. If it is still missing, email us with your platform, the date and the order or receipt ID from Apple or Google.

How do I restore Remove Ads on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings or in the Voucher Shop. Voucher spins are used up when delivered, so they can't be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone and iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history.

What are the odds for voucher spins?

Every spin shows its exact odds in the Voucher Shop before you buy, under the price and in Odds & details.

I saw an inappropriate ad

Use Report an ad in Settings, or email us with the time you saw it and a screenshot if you can. You can also change your ad privacy choices in Settings.

I found a bug

Email us with what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

Privacy questions are covered in the privacy policy.
