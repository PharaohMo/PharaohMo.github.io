Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There are no accounts or cloud saves. Vouchers and Instant Hatches you bought are part of your progress, so Google Play can't restore them; only Remove Ads can be restored.

Uninstalling the game deletes your progress from the device, but if Google backup is on, reinstalling the game can bring back your last backup, which might not have your most recent progress.

Moving to a new phone

Your progress can come along with your device's own backup or transfer, but this is not guaranteed.

When you set up the new phone, copy data directly from your old phone, or restore from your Google backup. Restoring a backup needs Google backup turned on in your old phone's settings, and backups run at most once a day, so your most recent play may be missing from one.

Progress can't move between Android and other kinds of device.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game while online: it checks for purchases that were paid for but not yet delivered, and delivers them. If it is still missing, email us with your device, the date and the order ID from Google Play.

How do I restore Remove Ads on a new device?

Sign in with the same Google account you bought it with, then tap Restore purchases in Settings or in the Voucher Shop. Voucher spins and Instant Hatches are used up when delivered, so the store can't restore them; they move only with your progress.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store; we can't issue them ourselves. Open your Google Play order history (Google's guide: Google Play refund policies).

A child bought something without permission

All purchases go through your Google account, so the refund request goes to the store: request a refund for the purchase from your Google Play order history. Google's refund policies explain the steps, including purchases a family member made by accident.

To approve future purchases, turn on purchase approval in Google Play and Family Link (Google Play: purchase approvals).

Managing spending

The Voucher Shop's Recent spins list shows your last 20 spins and the vouchers each one gave. Every purchase and its price is in your Google Play order history. Voucher spins are always optional, and nothing in the game is sold for a limited time.

What are the odds for voucher spins?

Every spin shows its exact odds in the Voucher Shop before you buy, under the price, in the confirmation before the store's payment sheet, and in Odds & details. The odds page lists the same odds in full: every spin, every egg tier, every lineage, prismatic eggs and hatch rolls, with a dated record of any change (확률형 아이템 정보 in Korean included).

I saw an inappropriate ad

Use Report an ad in Settings, or email us with the time you saw it and a screenshot if you can. You can also change your ad privacy choices in Settings.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).

I found a bug

Use Report a problem in Settings → Help: it opens an email with details that help us find the bug. Add what you were doing when it happened; a screenshot or screen recording helps a lot. If the game can't open your camp, use Email support on that screen.
