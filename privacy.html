Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Rogue Beastmaster app for iOS and Android ("the game") handles information. The game is made by PharaohDev ("we", "us"). If you have a question, email pharaohmo21@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Your progress is saved on your device, and your own device backups may include it.
  • We do not run servers, and we do not collect or sell your personal data. We only receive what you choose to email us.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and related data to show and measure ads, subject to the choices you make in the game and on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores the following on your phone or tablet:

  • your game progress, collection, eggs, vouchers, records and achievements;
  • your settings, such as battle speed and haptics;
  • a record of in-app purchases the game has delivered (the product and the store's transaction identifier, so a purchase is never granted twice) and whether you own Remove Ads.

The game also keeps a backup copy of your progress on your device, so it can recover if the saved copy is lost or damaged; it holds the same information. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it from your device, and you can erase your progress, including the backup copy, at any time with Reset all progress in the game's Settings. Your device's own backups and transfers can include it: iCloud and computer backups on iPhone and iPad, and Google backup and phone-to-phone transfer on Android (where the game limits backups to its saved progress). These are controlled by you and the platform, not by us, and are not guaranteed to include the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK from Google LLC ("AdMob"). Ads appear when you choose to watch one for a reward, and some may appear after a run ends. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising identifier (on iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with;
  • app, device and diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google is responsible for this processing. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in the game's Settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared, and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads shown after a run ends.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies. We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The store tells the game which product you bought and gives it a transaction identifier so the game can deliver the item; this is stored with your progress on your device. The odds for every random item are shown in the game before you buy and on our odds page. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. What you get when you buy is described in our Terms of Use.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to Google's PG (parental guidance) ad content rating: content suitable for most audiences with parental guidance. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share any information ourselves: the game does not send us any, and we do not share emails you send us. The third parties that may receive information when you play are Google (for ads, as described above) and Apple or Google (to deliver the app, updates and purchases, under their own policies).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, or to opt out of personalized advertising. Because we do not hold your data (other than emails you send us), you can exercise these rights with the in-game Privacy choices, your device settings, or directly with Google, Apple or the store you used. You can also contact us, and we will help you with your request. You may have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Your progress is stored on your device and is never sent to us. Device backups are kept by Apple or Google under their own security measures. Communication between the ads SDK and Google, and between the game and the app stores, is encrypted in transit.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: your progress and settings stay on your device until you erase them with Reset all progress or delete the game. The reset keeps the purchase record and Remove Ads, so a purchase is never delivered twice; deleting the game removes everything.
  • Device backups: a copy in your iCloud, computer or Google backup is kept and deleted according to your backup settings and the platform's policies. Resetting or deleting the game does not change a backup made earlier, so restoring that backup (or, on Android, reinstalling the game before the next backup) can bring the earlier progress back. You can delete backups in your device or account settings.
  • Emails to us: if you email us, including through Report an ad, Report a problem or Email support in the game, we receive your email address, your message and the details the game adds to it: the date, time and time zone, your device platform and system version, the game version and, for a problem report, your device type or model, which ads the build shows, the last save error and technical details about what the game was doing. We keep these emails only as long as we need them to help you, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Google and Apple: information that Google collects for ads, and that Apple or Google keep about your purchases, is retained under their own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game's release notes.
