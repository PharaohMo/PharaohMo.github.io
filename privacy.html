Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Rogue Beastmaster app for iOS and Android ("the game") handles information. The game is made by PharaohDev ("we", "us"). If you have a question, email pharaohmo21@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • We do not run servers, and we do not collect, sell or receive your personal data.
  • Versions of the game that show ads use Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and related data to show and measure ads, subject to the choices you make in the game and on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Test versions released up to and including 0.3.0 contain no ads and no purchases. This policy also covers later versions that add ads and in-app purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores the following only on your phone or tablet:

  • your game progress, collection, eggs, vouchers, records and achievements;
  • your settings, such as battle speed and haptics;
  • a record of in-app purchases the game has delivered (the product and the store's transaction identifier, so a purchase is never granted twice) and whether you own Remove Ads.

This information is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it. You can also erase your progress at any time in the game's Settings. Some platforms may include app data in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backups), which are controlled by you and the platform, not by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Versions of the game that show ads use the Google Mobile Ads SDK from Google LLC ("AdMob"). Ads appear when you choose to watch one for a reward, and some may appear after a run ends. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising identifier (on iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with;
  • app, device and diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google is responsible for this processing. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in the game's Settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared, and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads shown after a run ends.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies. We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The store tells the game which product you bought and gives it a transaction identifier so the game can deliver the item; this stays on your device. The odds for every random item are shown in the game before you buy. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share any information ourselves, because the game does not send us any. The third parties that may receive information when you play are Google (for ads, as described above) and Apple or Google (to deliver the app, updates and purchases, under their own policies).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, or to opt out of personalized advertising. Because we do not hold your data, you can exercise these rights with the in-game Privacy choices, your device settings, or directly with Google, Apple or the store you used. You can also contact us, and we will help you with your request. You may have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Your progress stays on your device. Communication between the ads SDK and Google, and between the game and the app stores, is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game's release notes.
